How a website and app grow your business

Today, people search online before they call, visit or buy. A website is your shop window, and a mobile app is a shortcut right on your customer's phone. Together they work every hour of the day. Scroll down and follow the journey of a business as it grows online.

☁️
☁️
☁️

Stage 1

Offline only

Customers find you only by word of mouth or by walking past. You are open during shop hours, and when people search online, they find your competitors first.

Stage 2

Your website goes live

Your business is now open 24 hours. Visitors can see your services, prices, photos, location and contact details at any time, without calling or visiting.

Stage 3

Found on Google

With SEO and fast loading, people searching for what you offer find you. These are new customers who had never heard of your business before.

Stage 4

Trust and leads grow

A professional site shows you are credible. Visitors check a website before they call or buy, and enquiry forms and WhatsApp buttons turn them into leads even while you sleep.

Stage 5

Sales move online

An online store, bookings and payments in your website or mobile app let you sell to customers beyond your city. Analytics show what works, so you can improve every month.

Stage 6

Your brand scales

Your website and mobile app become the base for ads, social media and repeat customers. They support new products and branches, and grow as your business grows.
